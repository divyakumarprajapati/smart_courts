Walk onto a court that greets you, officiates your game, and calls every line

2026 • Early-Stage Hardware + Software

The Problem

  • Manual scoring and refereeing errors → disputes and inconsistent enforcement
  • High dependency on qualified officials → expensive, limited availability
  • No real-time player insights → missed coaching and performance data
  • Fragmented systems → line calling, scoring, analytics require separate vendors
  • Unaffordable for small venues → $60K+/court for Hawk-Eye limits adoption

The Solution: CourtNG

An AI-first court operating system that fully automates game flow and officiating:

  • Autonomous Officiating: Detect rally start/end, score points, call fouls in real time
  • Sport-Agnostic Rules Engine: Configure for tennis, pickleball, badminton, futsal, etc.
  • Edge-First Deployment: On-premise inference, low latency, works offline
  • Integrated Analytics: Player stats, heatmaps, performance trends in real time
